About

I'm a geotechnical engineer based in London, and photography is the other half of how I look at places: landscapes and street scenes shot across Scotland, Spain, Germany and England, usually graded toward a colder, cinematic read.

I shoot for the same reason I got into engineering. I like understanding how a place actually holds together, and photographing it is a different way of paying that same attention.
